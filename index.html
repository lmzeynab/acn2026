Course Description

The Advanced Computer Networks course provides an in-depth study of modern networking principles, protocols, and services. Students explore advanced networking technologies, gain an understanding of research methodologies and emerging trends in computer networking, and develop the skills required to analyze and evaluate networked systems. The course combines theoretical concepts with hands-on implementation through laboratory exercises and projects on advanced networking topics, enabling students to design, implement, and assess contemporary computer networks.
